Equations of Motion

Read the three topics below in order.

TOPIC 1

Advanced Concepts

For motion with constant acceleration there are three equations of motion:


1. v = u + at

2. s = ut + ½at²

3. v² = u² + 2as

Symbols

u = initial velocity, v = final velocity, a = acceleration, t = time, s = displacement.

TOPIC 2

Applications

These equations are used to find the stopping distance of a vehicle, the time an object takes to fall, and the speed of a train after it speeds up.

Remember

In free fall, acceleration is g = 9.8 m/s² downward.

TOPIC 3

Problem Solving

Example: A car starts from rest (u = 0) and accelerates at 2 m/s² for 5 s.


Final velocity: v = 0 + 2 × 5 = 10 m/s

Distance: s = 0 + ½ × 2 × 5² = 25 m

Steps

Write the given values, choose the right equation, substitute, and check the units.
