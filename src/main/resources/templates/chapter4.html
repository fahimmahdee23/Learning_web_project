Applications and Review

Read the three topics below in order.

TOPIC 1

Applications

Seat belts and airbags protect people using Newton's First Law.


Brakes use friction to stop vehicles.


Sports like football and cricket depend on force and acceleration.


Rockets and jet engines work by Newton's Third Law.

Remember

Force and motion appear in almost everything we do every day.

TOPIC 2

Important Questions

1. Differentiate between distance and displacement.

2. State Newton's three laws of motion.

3. What is inertia? Give two examples.

4. A 10 kg object is pushed with 20 N. Find its acceleration.

5. Write the three equations of motion.

Answer to Question 4

a = F / m = 20 / 10 = 2 m/s²

TOPIC 3

Summary

Motion is a change of position with time.


Force changes motion, and F = ma links force, mass and acceleration.


Newton's three laws explain how and why things move.


The equations of motion let us calculate velocity, time and distance.
