What is Ohm's Law?

Ohm's Law explains the relationship between voltage, current, and resistance in an electrical circuit.

It states that the current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to voltage and inversely proportional to resistance, provided temperature remains constant.

Ohm's Law Formula

I = V / R
  • V = Voltage (Volts)
  • I = Current (Amperes)
  • R = Resistance (Ohms)

Example

Suppose a circuit has a voltage of 12 V and resistance of 6 Ω.

Current = Voltage / Resistance

Current = 12 / 6 = 2 A
